Install on your controller

  1. Tap Download latest app and choose your server.
  2. If the browser warns about the file, choose Download anyway.
  3. Open the downloaded file. If Android asks, allow installs from this source, then tap Install.
  4. Open Asset Inspector and sign in with your Drone Harmony account.
